How it works

Up and running in minutes.

No personal trainer needed. No complicated setup.

1

Tell Forma about yourself

Set your goal (strength, hypertrophy, fat loss), your fitness level, what equipment you have, and how many days a week you want to train. Takes about 2 minutes.

2

Get your complete program

Forma generates a full multi-week training plan with the right exercises, sets, reps, and starting weights — tailored to your exact situation.

3

Log your workouts, watch the weights climb

Tap to log each set. Forma automatically adds weight when you're ready to progress. Your only job is to show up.
